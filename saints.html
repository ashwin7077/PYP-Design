Our Saints

These revered individuals serve as beacons of divine guidance and unwavering faith. Their lives and teachings continue to shape the path of Yoga, Ayurveda and Sanatan Dharma today.

Maharishi Ved Vyas

He is also known as Krishna Dvaipayana. Around 5,000 years ago, he was born in Damauli of Tanahu district in Nepal. His father was Parashar Rishi, a sage, and his mother was Satyavati. Ved Vyas was the first and greatest acharya of Sanatan Dharma.

Bhanubhakta Acharya

Often called the “Aadikabi,” or first poet of the Nepali language, Bhanubhakta Acharya is celebrated for his translation of the epic Ramayana from Sanskrit to Nepali, a contribution with a lasting impact on Nepali literature and culture.

Read more

Gautama Buddha

Siddhartha Gautama, commonly known as the Buddha, is a foundational figure in Buddhism, revered for his teachings on enlightenment and the nature of suffering. His life and philosophy have had a profound impact on millions around the world.

Read more

Maharishi Manu Maharaj

According to Sanatan Dharma, Manu was the first man in the world, born about 19,700 years ago as Swayambhuva Manu, self-born, with the first woman, Shatarupa. All the people of this world are considered children of Manu, and are called Manav, or Manushya.

Read more

Acharya Ashtavakra

Known through the Ashtavakra Gita, a significant text within Advaita Vedanta, Acharya Ashtavakra is remembered for his profound dialogue with King Janaka exploring the nature of reality and self-awareness.

Read more

Maharishi Patanjali

A revered figure in ancient Indian philosophy, Maharishi Patanjali is known primarily for his contributions to yoga, grammar and medicine. His Yoga Sutras remain foundational to the development of classical yoga and the study of Sanskrit.

Read more

Maharishi Gautam

A prominent figure in Hindu mythology and philosophy, Maharishi Gautam is recognised as one of the Saptarishi, the Seven Great Sages. His contributions to Vedic literature and spirituality are significant, and many legends highlight his wisdom and virtue.

Read more

Maharishi Valmiki

Maharishi Valmiki was born Ratnakara, to Sage Prachetasa. Lost in the forest as a young man, he was raised by a hunter who found him, and grew up unaware of his origins, before going on to compose the epic Ramayana.

Raja Janak

King Janaka is a significant figure in Hindu mythology, best known within the Ramayana as the father of Sita, wife of Lord Rama. He is celebrated for his wisdom, spirituality and role as a just and enlightened ruler.
