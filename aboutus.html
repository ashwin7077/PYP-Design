Our Story

A Sacred Vow, Rooted in Service

Established on October 7, 2006, under the esteemed guidance of Param Pujya Yog Rishi Swami Ramdev Ji Maharaj and Param Shraddheya Ayurved Shiromani Acharya Balkrishna Ji Maharaj, Patanjali Yogpeeth Nepal is a non-profit organization dedicated to promoting health, spirituality, and prosperity through Yoga and Ayurveda. Registered under the Company Act of 2063, the organization has been instrumental in fostering a healthy, peaceful, and self-reliant Nepal.

From the temple towns of the Kathmandu valley to the remotest hill villages, our work carries one belief forward — that the ancient sciences of Yoga and Ayurveda are not relics of the past, but living tools for a healthier, more self-reliant tomorrow.

“A nation rich in herbal resources should use its herbal nature to cure, heal and rejuvenate its people — every youth empowered, a Yogi in complete awareness of his full strength.” Yog Rishi Swami Ramdev Ji Maharaj

What We Do

Our Path of Service

Five branches of one mission — healing, teaching, and standing with Nepal in its hardest moments.

Healthcare Services

Holistic Ayurvedic and Yogic treatment brought directly to communities across Nepal, from valley towns to remote hill villages.

Education

Patanjali Ayurved Medical College & Research Center in Dhulikhel offers BAMS degrees affiliated with Nepal Sanskrit University.

Disaster Relief

On the ground through the 2015 and 2023 earthquakes, the 2024 floods, and the COVID-19 pandemic.

Community Development

Yoga camps, health awareness drives and cultural programmes running across all 77 districts.

Social Welfare

Free education, housing and healthcare for orphans and underprivileged families, toward a society without discrimination.

Our Purpose

Eight Objectives

The founding commitments behind every programme we run. Pick one to read it.

Promotion of Yoga and Ayurveda

Establishing Yoga and Ayurveda as integral components of health care and wellness on a global scale.

Research and Study

Studying ancient manuscripts of the Vedic tradition, and conducting research that validates and modernises these practices.

Community Health Initiatives

Health camps and awareness programmes that teach the public about Yoga and natural methods of healing.

Support for the Underprivileged

Free accommodation, food and education for orphans and for economically weaker sections of society.

Educational Programs

Institutions offering courses in Yoga and Ayurveda, nurturing a new generation of practitioners.

Cultural Preservation

Carrying cultural values forward through Yoga and Ayurveda, for spiritual, physical and mental well-being alike.

Environmental Sustainability

Organic farming and herbal medicine that give back to the land they draw from.

National Health Improvement

Weaving traditional practice into modern health systems to lift Nepal's overall health landscape.
