The Yoga Sutras of Patanjali
अथ योगानुशासनम्
atha yogānuśāsanam

“Now begins the teaching of yoga.” So opens the first of 195 sūtras — threads of wisdom, composed by the sage Patanjali, that map the path from a restless mind to the stillness in which the true Self is known.
