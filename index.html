Patanjali Yogpeeth · Main Entrance

Since 2006

A gateway to health, spirituality and prosperity.

Established on October 7, 2006, under the esteemed guidance of Param Pujya Yog Rishi Swami Ramdev Ji Maharaj and Param Shraddheya Ayurved Shiromani Acharya Balkrishna Ji Maharaj, Patanjali Yogpeeth Nepal is a non-profit organization dedicated to promoting health, spirituality, and prosperity through Yoga and Ayurveda. Registered under the Company Act of 2063, the organization has been instrumental in fostering a healthy, peaceful, and self-reliant Nepal.

Some formulations now have modern pharmacological studies backing their effects — Ashwagandha for stress, turmeric for inflammation. It's evidence-based in parts, though not all claims are validated by modern clinical trials — that's an active, ongoing research area.

Allopathy typically targets symptoms quickly — a painkiller stops pain fast. Ayurveda aims to correct the root imbalance in the body (dosha), which takes time since it works with the body's natural healing process rather than overriding it.
