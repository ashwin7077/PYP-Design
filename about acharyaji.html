Acharya Balkrishna is a dynamic personality who manages the whole cluster of Patanjali units in coordination with their heads, and is admired above all for a rare combination: listening patiently, talking little, and working hard in the interest of the general masses.

Early life and training प्रारंभिक जीवन एवं शिक्षा

He was educated in the Guru-Shishya Parampara at the Gurukul at Kalwa, in Jind, Haryana, under the guidance of the late Acharya Shri Baldevji, and has never looked back since. He is a great visionary — highly ascetic, energetic, diligent, simple and easy-going — a versatile personality with multi-dimensional skills, constantly engaged in the service of mankind from the core of his heart.

Acharya Balkrishna is known to be a silent worker with a sober behaviour, possessing the qualities that set him apart from others: patience, restraint in speech, and a readiness to work more than he claims.

Scholarship and Ayurveda आयुर्वेद एवं विद्वता

He is a great scholar of Ayurveda, Sanskrit and the Vedas, well versed with Sāṅkhya Yoga, Ayurveda, the Sanskrit language, Panini's Aṣṭādhyāyī, the Upanishads and the richness of Indian cultural philosophy. He carries forward ancient lifestyle, tradition and natural healing, and through his effort Ayurvedic culture has become acceptable to modern life once again — a role model to a younger generation.

His initiatives for biodiversity conservation are unparalleled: he has explored Sanjeevani, Soma, Swarnaksiri, Swarnadraka and the Aṣṭavarga plants, each on the verge of extinction. His work in characterising medicinal plants and authenticating indigenous medicine at national and international levels speaks to the depth of his knowledge. He is presently engaged in projects of national and international importance — herbal gardens and herbariums, the first-ever World Botanical Dictionary, the Wealth of Food Crops, and a vegetation survey of India.

Himalayan expeditions हिमालय अभियान

In September 2022, Acharya Ji mounted three unmounted, inaccessible peaks of the Himalaya, discovering many rare herbs along the way and naming them in the Sanatan tradition. A year later, in September 2023, he climbed two unnamed and unclimbed icebergs in the Harsil region of the Himalaya. The first peak, standing 17,500 ft high, was named "OM Kailash Shikhar"; the second, at 16,500 ft, was named "Nandi Shikhar."

Reach of the work जनसेवा

15 lakh+

More than 1,500 Ayurveda doctors presently provide free primary healthcare and guidance to over 70 lakh patients a year. He is also the most-followed entrepreneur on Indian social media, with more than ten million followers across platforms.

Institutions शिक्षा संस्थान

With a vision for meaningful, quality education, Acharya Balkrishna has established educational institutions including an Ayurvedic college and university, built to educate children of every caste and creed toward their full potential — so that they, in turn, contribute to their communities and to the world's wellbeing. More than ten lakh community members have benefited from this work.

  • Ayurvedic College & University
  • Herbal gardens and herbariums
  • World Botanical Dictionary (in progress)

Writings and publications साहित्य एवं प्रकाशन

He has published more than 441 research articles in national and international journals, authored more than 200 books across Yoga, Ayurveda, agriculture and information technology, and edited more than 40 previously unpublished ancient Ayurveda manuscripts. To strengthen the world's traditional medicine systems, he published the multi-volume World Herbal Encyclopedia, describing 60,000 medicinal plant species alongside the world's largest collection of medicinal plant paintings.

  • Aushadh Darshan Sold more than 1 crore copies
  • The Science of Ayurveda Published in 71 languages worldwide
  • World Herbal Encyclopedia 60,000 medicinal plant species, multi-volume

Humanitarian response मानवीय सेवा

Close to humanity above all, he has played a significant role in assessing post-disaster needs and coordinating with government authorities to address them quickly — work that has saved lives in the immediate aftermath of catastrophe. His relief efforts following the 2004 tsunami, the 2008 Bihar floods, the 2013 Uttarakhand disaster and the 2015 Nepal earthquake are widely noted.
