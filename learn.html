Learn basic yoga and pranayam

Where
At home
Time
10 to 20 minutes a day
You need
No experience

Start with the foundation. Learn a few simple postures and the breathing practices that go with them. You can begin at home, at your own pace.

Choose your first lesson

Basic yoga

Simple standing, sitting and lying postures, explained one at a time.

Open basic yoga

Pranayam

Breathing techniques that calm the mind and build lung strength.

Open pranayam

Before your first practice

  • A mat or folded blanket
  • Loose, comfortable clothes
  • Early morning, on an empty stomach
  • The same time every day

Go to the yoga centre

Where
Your nearest centre
Time
Regular group classes
You need
Steady home practice

A book or video can show you a posture. A teacher can correct it. Join a class at your nearest centre to practise in a group and build a steady routine.

What a centre gives you

  • Learn correct posture and alignment from a trained teacher.
  • Practise with others, so it is easier to stay regular.
  • Ask questions about your own body and your own needs.
Find a yoga centre

Be an assistant yoga teacher

Where
Your local centre
Time
After some months of practice
You need
Regular attendance

After some months of regular practice, you can begin helping in class. Working beside your teacher is the best way to learn how yoga is taught.

Your role in class

Demonstrate

Show postures so new students can see them.

Guide

Help beginners with breathing and body position.

Observe

Learn how a class is planned, paced and closed.

You do not need to be perfect. You need to be regular, patient and willing to keep learning.

Come to Haridwar and learn advanced yoga

Where
Patanjali Yogpeeth, Haridwar
Time
A residential stay
You need
A steady practice

Haridwar is the home of Patanjali Yogpeeth. Spend time here to go deeper, with experienced teachers and time away from daily distractions.

What you study

Advanced practice

Advanced asanas and pranayam, practised with guidance.

Meditation and philosophy

Meditation, and the philosophy of the Patanjali Yoga Sutra.

Daily discipline

A disciplined daily routine that strengthens your own practice.

Open a Yog Sadhana Kendra and teach yoga

Where
Your own community
Time
A regular morning class
You need
Support from your Yog Samiti

Bring what you have learned back to your community. Open a Yog Sadhana Kendra, start with a small morning batch, and teach your neighbours. This is how yoga keeps spreading.

How to begin

  1. Start small

    Begin with a small, regular morning class.

  2. Teach and keep learning

    Teach what you have mastered, and keep learning as you teach.

  3. Stay connected

    Stay in touch with your local Patanjali Yog Samiti for support.

Your first step is one breath away

Choose where to begin. Both lessons take only a few minutes a day, and you can start this morning.
